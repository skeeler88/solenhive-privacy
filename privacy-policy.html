SolenHive Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-07-28

Summary

SolenHive is an offline-first market exploration app. We do not create user accounts and we do not sell personal data. Market history and news headlines are fetched from third-party public sources when you request them, and watchlist data is stored on your device.

Who we are

SolenHive (“the app”, “we”) is a market data exploration tool for education and research.

Privacy contact: sanders.skyler@gmail.com
Developer name: SolenHive

Data we do not collect

The app does not require sign-in. We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, precise location, contacts, photos, payment card data, or government IDs through the app. We do not show ads and do not include advertising SDKs.

Data processed on your device

Symbols you type into a watchlist, lookback choices, and downloaded price history are stored locally in app storage so you can use the app offline. You can clear app storage through your device settings to remove this data.

Network requests (third parties)

When you download history or refresh news tone, the app sends HTTPS requests that may include ticker symbols and date ranges to:

  1. NASDAQ public historical quote endpoints for daily bars
  2. Google News RSS search for article titles used only for on-device headline-tone scoring

Those providers process requests under their own terms and privacy policies. We do not operate an intermediate SolenHive backend that stores your queries.

Permissions (Android)

INTERNET and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE only. We do not request contacts, microphone, camera, SMS, or location.

Children

SolenHive is not directed at children under 13 (or the equivalent age in your region).

Security

Network traffic uses HTTPS. Local files stay on your device. No method of transmission or storage is perfectly secure.

Retention and deletion

Because there is no account, there is no cloud account to delete. Uninstall the app or clear app storage to remove local caches. Third-party servers may retain standard web logs under their policies.

Changes

We may update this policy. The “Last updated” date will change when we do.

Not investment advice

SolenHive does not provide personalized investment advice, brokerage, or order execution. Scores, outlooks, and screens are educational models only and are not recommendations to buy or sell.
